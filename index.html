How to play

One real price chart with the name removed. You see 60 bars of history, then you trade the next 40 — one bar at a time.

  1. Choose a position: up to 2× long or 2× short, or stay flat.
  2. The next bar reveals and your equity moves.
  3. Changing position costs 5bp, so flip-flopping bleeds you.
  4. After 40 bars you find out what you were trading.

Hit Auto to let the bars run on a timer instead of clicking through — you keep changing position while it plays.

Turn on moving averages under Study, or draw your own trendlines straight onto the chart. Drawings stay anchored to price as the chart rescales.

Everyone gets the same chart each day. Beat buy & hold to keep your streak.

Difficulty

Changes which pool of charts the daily puzzle is drawn from. Everyone on the same difficulty gets the same chart.
